### Findings

- B-T01 · P1 · footer · Taste · No Blog link anywhere in the footer (known gap), so the 19 articles are reachable only from the URL · add Blog to the Company column.
- B-T02 · P1 · footer (every page) · Taste (button-styles, one system) · The six popular-search chips are white outlined pills, counted as a third button style beside the primary and the header's Quote · set them as plain footer links in the footer's one link look.
- B-T03 · P1 · /about · Taste (copy-headline) · The h1 "About AIMS Security Systems" names no category; only the lead does · name ZKTeco and the place in the h1 (EN and AR).
- B-T04 · P1 · /support · Taste (copy-headline) · The h1 "Support and warranty" names no product · "ZKTeco support and warranty" (EN and AR).
- B-T05 · P1 · /blog · Taste (copy-headline) · The h1 "Guides from AIMS" names no subject · name access control and POS in the h1 (EN and AR); the meta title stays.
- B-T06 · P1 · /blog · Taste (cta) · The index has no action in the intro and ends on the card grid with no way forward · close the page with the shared closing band (quote action, contact link), as About, Services and Support do.
- B-T07 · P1 · /about, /services at 640–1023 · Taste (product big; hero-fill at 768) · The stacked hero shows AIMS's product at 16rem (about 120px wide on a 768 screen) and the next section peeks into the first screen · on the company pages, a larger photo on tablets so the product reads big and the hero fills the screen.
- B-T08 · P2 · /about · Taste (heading-scale) · The showrooms section's h2 "Visit or call" is 24px beside 38px section headings · on About, set it at the section-heading size (company.css, the shared LocationCards unchanged).
- B-T09 · P2 · articles · Taste (copy-headings) · "More from AIMS" names nothing · "More articles from AIMS" (EN and AR).
- B-T10 · P2 · articles, legal · Taste (heading-scale) · h2 in three sizes (AIMS's in-article 20px, the "Plan it" card 24px, "More from AIMS" 38px) · keep: AIMS's article headings sit inside the 680px reading column; the page's own section heading is a different level of the page. Not changed.
- B-T11 · P2 · articles at 390 · Taste (section-gaps) · 106px from the last section to the footer against a 54px median · trim the "More articles" bottom padding to one section space.
- B-T12 · P2 · footer · Taste (text-contrast) · The wordmark's lime full stop reads 2.14:1 on paper-2 · waived with the reason already used for the header's wordmark (a decorative mark, not text).
- B-T13 · P2 · articles at 1440 · Taste (overlap-text) · "More from AIMS" reported over the sticky index's entries · not reproduced: probed at three scroll positions, the index stays inside its layout (bottom 56px above the heading). Checker artefact of its tall viewport; no change.
- B-T14 · P1 · /blog, articles · Taste (focus-ring, focus-visible) · `.ed-card a:focus-visible { outline: none }`; the ring moves to the card via `:has()`, which the checker cannot see · verify in the Impeccable pass (keyboard), then keep the card ring and draw it on the stretched link so the rule that removes the outline puts one back.
