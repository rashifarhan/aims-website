### Findings

- B-I01 · P1 · footer (AR) · Impeccable (harden, RTL) · "© 2026 AIMS Security Systems Trading LLC." and the English showroom address reorder inside Arabic lines ("AIMS Security Systems Trading 2026 © LLC.", "· Deira, Dubai" split from its address) · isolate both English runs with `dir="ltr"` (CLAUDE.md §7).
- B-I02 · P1 · legal, Support, articles · Impeccable (typeset, Read) · AIMS's text runs ~88–93 characters a line at 16px in the 680px column (aim 65–75) · set the reading body at 18px (`--text-lg`) from 640px, so the 680px column (Design Doc 10.1) holds ~75 characters; headings step up to keep their contrast with the body; phones stay 16px.
- B-I03 · P1 · /blog · Impeccable (optimize) · The first card's image is the LCP element on phones and desktop but loads `lazy` · preload the first card's image only (CLAUDE.md §5: only the LCP image gets priority).
- B-I04 · P2 · /blog · Impeccable (optimize) · Card `sizes` says 400px at ≥1024 while cards render 409px · 420px.
- B-I05 · P2 · articles · Impeccable (distill; detector nested-cards) · "Plan it with AIMS" is a bordered green panel holding a white card and a button: a card in a card · keep the panel, drop the inner card's box: the related solution becomes a plain link block.
- B-I06 · P2 · /blog/[slug] (AR) · Impeccable (adapt, RTL) · AIMS's English title is `dir="ltr"`, so on Arabic pages it sits on the left while the breadcrumb, eyebrow and date sit on the right · align it to the Arabic page's start edge (`text-align: end` in its own LTR direction).
- B-I07 · P2 · /blog, articles · Impeccable (harden, keyboard) · Card focus: the ring is drawn on the card via `:has()` and is visible (screenshot), but the link rule only removes the outline · also underline the title on focus, so the focused link itself changes (and the Taste checker sees it; B-T14).
- B-I08 · P2 · articles · Impeccable (harden, keyboard) · The related-solution card's focus ring circles only "Door locks →" while its stretched link covers the whole card · ring the card, as ArticleCard does.
- B-I09 · P2 · footer at 640–1023 · Impeccable (adapt) · The contact list fills half the width with nothing beside it above the four columns · not changed: it reads as one block and the columns follow at once; a two-column contact list would split the phones from their labels.
- B-I10 · P2 · /services · Impeccable (detector icon-tile-stack) · 52px icon tiles above each service heading · the shared `SolutionOptions` card (agent C's template): for the integrator, not changed here.
- B-I11 · P2 · footer (phones) · Impeccable (a11y) · Each folded group's `h2` sits inside its `summary`, where some screen readers drop the heading role · not changed: the `nav` keeps its label, and the desktop columns keep their headings.
